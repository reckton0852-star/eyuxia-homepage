ESP32 / LVGL / Cloudflare

1.47 inch Market Ticker

v2.1.3 OTA verified

A compact market data display built on the Waveshare ESP32-S3-LCD-1.47B. It shows selected US stock quotes, supports browser-based configuration, uses a cloud proxy for stable data delivery, and keeps the last successful quote on screen during refresh.

Display 1.47 inch LCD
Controls 2 buttons + RGB LED
Config Hotspot + browser setup
Network Wi-Fi + proxy architecture
View Product Page Open Repository Release v2.1.3 OTA verified

What it solves: Turns a simple price-display demo into a stable desk device with boot states, Wi-Fi recovery, setup mode, and cloud-backed refresh behavior.

What I learned: Product quality comes from startup behavior, configuration, recovery, and physical interaction, not just the core feature itself.

AI / Voice / Feishu Workflow

AI Meeting Recorder

In Progress

An office-focused smart hardware concept for meeting rooms. The device is designed to capture meeting audio, transcribe it, upload the transcript into Feishu Docs, and use large language models to generate meeting minutes, action items, and summaries.

Office Feishu Speech to Text LLM Workflow
01 Capture meeting audio
02 Transcribe speech into text
03 Sync output to Feishu Docs
04 Generate minutes, tasks, and summaries

Why this project: A personal alternative to recurring meeting-recorder subscriptions, shaped as a dedicated office-room device.

Current stage: Defining the hardware form, audio chain, transcription approach, and Feishu automation workflow.

ESP32-S3 / 3.16 inch RGB / Market Data

Reckton Market Tile

In Development

A wider, color-first desktop market companion designed for two-second readability. It brings price, daily movement, trend, market state, and data freshness into one glanceable 820 x 320 view, while preserving useful quotes through network or service interruptions.

3.16 inch RGB US Stocks + ETFs Browser Setup USB-C Powered
01 Boot and restore saved configuration
02 Connect to Wi-Fi or open local setup
03 Fetch compact quotes through the cloud proxy
04 Show a glanceable market scene and freshness state
View Product Page

Product direction: A dependable desk object for people who follow a small watchlist without repeatedly opening a phone or trading app.

Current stage: Display, PSRAM, Wi-Fi setup, quote delivery, caching, and button foundations are working. The new Market and Focus scenes are being designed and validated.

About

Product-minded, hardware-curious.

This lab approaches hardware from a product manager's point of view: start with the use case, then build the firmware, screen UI, setup path, cloud integration, enclosure, and iteration loop around it.

It is part learning journal, part prototype archive, and part long-term attempt to turn small experiments into devices that feel considered, useful, and genuinely usable.
