ESP32-S3 / 3.16 inch RGB / Product Development

Reckton Market Tile

A color desktop market companion designed to make a small watchlist understandable in two seconds. Market Tile combines price, daily movement, trend, market state, and data freshness in one wide display, without asking the user to open a phone or keep a finance app in view.

Explore the Product Direction See the 1.47 inch Predecessor

Product Role

Market awareness that lives quietly on the desk.

Market Tile is not a trading terminal and it is not a miniature finance app. It is a persistent, glanceable information surface for people who follow a focused list of US stocks and ETFs while working at a desk.

V1 is being built as a USB-C powered prototype around the Waveshare ESP32-S3-LCD-3.16. The product direction is confirmed; the display, networking, setup, cloud quote path, caching, and physical-button foundations are working, while the final Market and Focus scenes remain under active development.

V1 Product Experience

One display, two levels of attention.

V1 intentionally focuses on US stocks and ETFs before expanding into additional asset classes.

Market Scene

The default glance view combines symbol, company name, current price, daily change, trend, market status, quote time, freshness state, and watchlist position.

Focus Scene

A deliberate second view expands the trend and daily range for closer inspection, without turning the display into a dense trading dashboard.

Alert Scene

A planned temporary state will surface meaningful price or movement thresholds, then return to the normal market view after acknowledgement.

Freshness First

LIVE, DELAYED, STALE, and OFFLINE states are designed to make the age and reliability of displayed data explicit.

Last Good Quote

Background refresh never clears a useful quote. If Wi-Fi or the service fails, the previous successful data remains visible with an honest status.

Setup Without an App

Saved Wi-Fi networks and the watchlist are configured through a local browser portal, preserving recovery without reflashing firmware.

Interaction Model

Simple controls for a device meant to stay in view.

Single Click Next symbol
Long Press Previous symbol
Double Click Switch between Market and Focus scenes
Hold for 3 Seconds Enter local setup mode
After Manual Input Pause automatic watchlist rotation for 30 seconds

System Architecture

A proven device foundation with a wider product layer.

Market Tile reuses the reliable architecture learned from the 1.47 inch Market Ticker while keeping the 3.16 inch display driver independent. The device owns display, local setup, controls, caching, and recovery. A cloud proxy owns API isolation, compact payloads, and service compatibility.

01 Boot and restore

Initialize the ST7701 display and PSRAM, then restore saved Wi-Fi, watchlist, brightness, and rotation settings.

02 Connect or recover

Try known 2.4 GHz networks; if none can connect, expose a local setup hotspot and browser portal.

03 Request device-ready quotes

Use the existing stock.eyuxia.xyz proxy path so credentials and provider-specific parsing stay off the ESP32.

04 Render an honest state

Update the complete market model after a successful response, or preserve the last usable quote and mark its freshness.
